# Contributing

## Definition of done

A complete system has:

- a unique ID, owner, purpose, audience, and bounded scope;
- a documented trigger or use condition;
- roles and responsibilities;
- inputs, outputs, steps or decision logic;
- exceptions, escalation, or failure handling where relevant;
- reusable artifacts or templates;
- version and review dates;
- evidence that it has been tested, approved, or used successfully;
- links to related systems without duplicating their content.

## Completion scoring

Score ten points for each satisfied definition-of-done area above. Use the score to prioritize work. `complete` and `maintenance` require a score of at least 80 and recorded evidence.

## Review cadence

- Review active systems at least annually.
- Review systems sooner after a major platform, policy, owner, or process change.
- Open an issue when a review finds a material gap.
- Preserve IDs and history when retiring or replacing a system.

## Pull requests

Describe the reason, affected IDs, status or score changes, evidence added, and any Notion fields that require refresh. Never include restricted organizational content.
