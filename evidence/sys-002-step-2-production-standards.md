## Conclusion

Step 2, “Close production standards and template gaps,” is complete at the EnablementOps operating-system layer. The step establishes selection, specification, tool, handoff, fallback, and lifecycle controls. It does not duplicate LACE build kits or claim that every family has a finished editable master.

Future build-kit expansion is controlled maintenance within LACE and does not reopen this gate unless a material change creates an uncovered production risk.
