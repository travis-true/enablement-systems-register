# SYS-003 EngagementOps Step 1 completion evidence

Step 1 is complete.

EngagementOps is registered as `SYS-003`, a distinct optional operating system. Its controlled foundation defines:

- the boundary between capability enablement and audience engagement;
- seven activation conditions and explicit non-triggers;
- the minimum activation record;
- decision rights across SYS-003, SYS-002, SYS-001, WF-002, domain authorities, and human approvers;
- three governed handoffs;
- ten hard-stop conditions;
- deactivation and sustainment requirements;
- controlled terminology that prevents awareness, participation, conversion, capability, and impact from being treated as equivalent.

The design preserves EngagementOps as trigger-based rather than always-on, prevents it from overriding source or release authority, and keeps confidential organizational implementation details outside the public repository.

Remaining work begins with governance, lifecycle, metadata, source-of-truth, privacy, accessibility, and approval standards.
