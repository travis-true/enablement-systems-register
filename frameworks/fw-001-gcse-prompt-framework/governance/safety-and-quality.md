# Safety and quality controls

## Shared controls

Every GCSE prompt should:

- preserve the legitimate purpose of the task;
- use a real source or an actionable source placeholder;
- define the response's format and quality criteria;
- require important missing or conflicting information to be identified;
- prohibit invented facts, decisions, dates, quotations, formulas, owners, deadlines, citations, policies, or source content when relevant;
- minimize confidential, proprietary, health-related, security-related, and unnecessary personal information; and
- keep consequential decisions with a responsible human reviewer.

Prefer aggregated data, de-identified data, sanitized excerpts, fictional examples, or bracketed placeholders when sensitive inputs are unnecessary.

## People-related work

Base prompts on observable facts, documented actions, established expectations, measurable outcomes, support needs, and neutral next steps. Do not ask a system to diagnose a person, infer protected characteristics or motives, judge personal worth, or make unsupported employment conclusions.

## Source priority

Define a task-specific source order when materials conflict. A suitable default is:

1. current final authoritative source;
2. current published supporting guidance;
3. approved reusable examples;
4. recent records such as transcripts or meeting notes;
5. drafts or format references.

Do not describe draft material as authoritative. Record unresolved conflicts instead of silently choosing or combining claims.

## Required human review

Before using or sharing a response:

- verify names, dates, calculations, quotations, and factual claims;
- compare the response with the named source;
- confirm the response meets Expectation;
- check that sensitive information was handled appropriately; and
- revise or stop when the source does not support the result.
