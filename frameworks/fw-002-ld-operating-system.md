# FW-002 — L&D Operating System

## Purpose

Provide governed lifecycle control for training and enablement work.

## Core capabilities

Intake, analysis, prioritization, roles, design, development, review, release, measurement, maintenance, and retirement. The detailed implementation and governed IDs remain in the [L-D-OS repository](https://github.com/travis-true/L-D-OS).

## Boundary

This register records the system's portfolio status; it does not duplicate its authoritative operating documentation.
