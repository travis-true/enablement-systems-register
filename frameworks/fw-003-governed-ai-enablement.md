# FW-003 — Governed AI Enablement Framework

## Purpose

Coordinate responsible AI adoption across governance, role-based use cases, practice, reinforcement, and measurement.

## Current gap

The public-safe framework needs explicit decision rights, common measures, pilot evidence, and a reusable adoption map. Employer-specific policies and operational details remain in approved internal storage.

## Completion target

A public derivative with defined roles, lifecycle stages, measures, exceptions, and evidence from at least one sanitized pilot.
