# PB-001 — Employee Training Reference Production

## Purpose

Turn source material into a publication-ready employee reference and a copy-ready modern SharePoint version.

## Playbook

1. Clarify audience, platform, license, interface date, exclusions, required guidance, support contacts, and deliverables.
2. Summarize the confirmed requirements and obtain approval to begin.
3. Inventory source content and screenshot needs.
4. Draft detailed procedures, examples, decision cues, and troubleshooting.
5. Apply the approved brand and accessibility rules when authorized.
6. Place and crop screenshots without hiding required interface context.
7. Produce the editable document and SharePoint content/layout instructions.
8. Render, inspect, repair, and record anything not independently verified.

## Completion evidence

Repeated production use has validated the intake, approval, screenshot, document, SharePoint, and QA stages.
