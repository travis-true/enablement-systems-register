# PB-003 — 30-Day AI Adoption Plan

## Purpose

Move a defined group from AI awareness to role-based, measured, reinforced use in thirty days.

## Draft phases

- Week 1: readiness, guardrails, baseline, and use-case selection.
- Week 2: guided practice with approved sources and prompts.
- Week 3: workflow application, peer examples, and manager support.
- Week 4: reinforcement, measurement, reflection, and next-cycle decisions.

## Completion gaps

Define daily or weekly actions, responsible roles, manager checkpoints, measures, reusable templates, exceptions, and completion evidence.
