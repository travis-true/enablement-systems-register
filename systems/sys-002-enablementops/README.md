# SYS-002 — EnablementOps

## Classification

EnablementOps is an **operating system**: the governed, end-to-end model for running an enablement function. It is not a single framework, workflow, or playbook.

## Purpose

Coordinate how enablement work is requested, prioritized, designed, produced, delivered, measured, maintained, and governed so people can perform required work successfully.

## Primary outcome

People can perform the required work with the appropriate capability, resources, support, and reinforcement.

## Scope

EnablementOps governs:

- needs and performance analysis;
- intake, triage, prioritization, and approval;
- learning and performance-support strategy;
- asset design, production, publication, and discoverability;
- delivery, practice, reinforcement, and support;
- measurement, evaluation, and improvement;
- ownership, lifecycle, versioning, maintenance, and retirement;
- system integration, automation, quality, accessibility, and governance.

## System boundary

EnablementOps owns capability and performance enablement. It may activate EngagementOps when work requires audience awareness, launch communications, participation, campaign orchestration, or ongoing engagement. EngagementOps remains a separate optional operating system and does not become mandatory scope for every EnablementOps initiative.

## Component model

EnablementOps can contain or coordinate:

1. Frameworks — principles and decision structures.
2. Workflows — ordered movement from trigger to outcome.
3. Playbooks — reusable operating guidance.
4. Standards and controls — mandatory requirements and decision rights.
5. Templates, schemas, tools, and automation — repeatable implementation assets.
6. Evidence and measures — proof of quality, use, outcomes, and maintenance.
