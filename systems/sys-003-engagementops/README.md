# SYS-003 — EngagementOps

## Classification

EngagementOps is an **optional operating system** for planning, governing, delivering, and measuring audience activation and sustained engagement. It is not a mandatory stage of every EnablementOps initiative.

## Purpose

Coordinate how defined audiences become aware, choose to participate, respond to calls to action, and remain engaged long enough to support an approved enablement or organizational outcome.

## Primary outcome

The intended audience receives relevant, accessible touchpoints and takes the desired engagement action without confusing attention or participation with demonstrated capability.

## Scope

EngagementOps governs:

- audience and stakeholder segmentation;
- awareness, launch, activation, and reinforcement strategy;
- campaign and touchpoint sequencing;
- channel selection and channel-specific requirements;
- calls to action, participation paths, and feedback loops;
- engagement calendars, coordination, and frequency controls;
- participation and engagement measures;
- campaign-asset metadata, lifecycle, maintenance, and retirement;
- engagement-specific accessibility, privacy, approval, and governance.

## Boundary

EnablementOps (`SYS-002`) owns capability and performance enablement. EngagementOps owns attention, activation, participation, and sustained engagement when those outcomes require coordinated touchpoints.

EngagementOps does not own source truth, learning outcomes, instructional design, technical accuracy, asset construction, publication authority, or proof that a person can perform the required work.
