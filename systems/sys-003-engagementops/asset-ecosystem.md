# EngagementOps reusable asset ecosystem

Select the smallest effective ecosystem that can achieve the approved engagement objective. More assets and channels increase coordination, accessibility, privacy, fatigue, maintenance, and measurement cost.

| Ecosystem | Typical components | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| What's New launch kit | Social post, carousel, micro-video, FAQ | A defined audience needs concise awareness and a clear launch action |
| Enablement campaign | Manager kit, champion kit, engagement calendar | Leaders or champions must contextualize and coordinate participation |
| Workflow reinforcement | GIF walkthrough, microlearning series, knowledge asset or job aid | An approved workflow needs repeated point-of-need reinforcement |
| Sustainment | Recurring social series, challenge, office hours, feedback loop | Continued attention or participation has defined ownership and exit criteria |

Each selected component requires an asset specification and must reference a controlled campaign and channel profile. Unsupported one-off outputs are not a substitute for the governed campaign record.

F0–F3 fidelity:

- F0 — presentation may be adapted without changing controlled meaning.
- F1 — sequence and decision points must be preserved; presentation may change within approved meaning.
- F2 — meaning-sensitive content requires named SME review.
- F3 — policy, legal, compliance, security, privacy, or high-consequence content requires exact preservation or explicit specialist approval.
