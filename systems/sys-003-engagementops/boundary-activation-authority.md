# EngagementOps boundary, activation, and authority

Version: 0.1.0  
Effective date: 2026-09-03  
Owner: Travis True  
Status: Approved for SYS-003 development

## Operating boundary

| Area | Governing authority |
|---|---|
| Capability, performance outcome, learning strategy, and enablement need | EnablementOps (`SYS-002`) |
| Engagement objective, audience activation, touchpoint sequence, channel mix, participation path, and engagement measures | EngagementOps (`SYS-003`) |
| Learning-asset construction, rendering, repair, and asset-specific QA | LACE (`SYS-001`) |
| Governed asset publication | Governed Training Asset Publication (`WF-002`) |
| Source claims, policy meaning, technical accuracy, and restricted decisions | Authorized content, technical, policy, privacy, or compliance owner |
| Final campaign activation, external distribution, and exceptions | Authorized human approver |

EngagementOps may support an EnablementOps initiative, but neither system absorbs the other. Shared records are linked by identifier rather than duplicated.

## Activation rule

EngagementOps activates only when an approved initiative requires one or more of these outcomes:

1. create awareness before or alongside enablement;
2. reach a defined audience through coordinated channels;
3. prompt registration, attendance, enrollment, contribution, or another explicit action;
4. manage a launch or time-bound engagement campaign;
5. reinforce attention or participation across multiple touchpoints;
6. collect engagement feedback or measure audience response;
7. sustain an audience relationship after initial delivery.

A single necessary operational notice, routine asset publication, or learning deliverable does not automatically activate EngagementOps.

## Required activation record

Before work begins, the requesting owner must provide:

- initiative and related-system identifiers;
- approved business or enablement outcome;
- engagement objective and observable call to action;
- intended and excluded audiences;
- timing, required milestones, and known dependencies;
- source owner and authoritative materials;
- available channels and channel owners;
- privacy, accessibility, legal, policy, brand, and localization constraints;
- approver and decision deadline;
- proposed measures and available baseline;
- sustainment or exit expectation.

Materially missing information returns the request for clarification. Urgency does not waive required authority or risk controls.

## Decision rights

| Decision | Accountable authority |
|---|---|
| Request EngagementOps activation | Initiative or EnablementOps owner |
| Accept, return, or decline activation | EngagementOps owner |
| Approve engagement objective, audience, call to action, sequence, and measures | EngagementOps owner with initiative owner |
| Approve factual, policy, technical, privacy, legal, compliance, or brand content | Authorized domain owner |
| Select learning or performance-support assets | EnablementOps owner |
| Construct governed learning assets | LACE under approved specifications |
| Approve publication or campaign launch | Authorized human approver |
| Approve material exception | Authority responsible for the affected control |
| Pause, correct, or withdraw an active campaign | EngagementOps owner or relevant risk authority |
| End, transition, or sustain the engagement | EngagementOps owner with initiative owner |

Technical access, tool capability, elapsed time, silence, or prior approval for another version never creates authority.

## Core handoffs

### EnablementOps to EngagementOps

SYS-002 supplies the approved outcome, audience need, source authority, required behaviors, constraints, dependencies, and success relationship. SYS-003 returns the engagement strategy, touchpoint plan, channel requirements, calls to action, measures, and sustainment decision.

### EngagementOps to LACE

SYS-003 supplies an approved asset brief and channel requirements. SYS-001 governs construction and asset-specific QA. EngagementOps cannot bypass LACE controls by labeling an asset as campaign content.

### EngagementOps to publication

SYS-003 supplies approved assets, channel metadata, schedule, audience, accessibility evidence, permissions, and approver record. Publication remains blocked until the applicable publication authority accepts the handoff.

## Hard stops

Work must stop, return, or escalate when:

- the objective, audience, call to action, source authority, or approver is materially unclear;
- requested wording invents or changes policy, compliance, technical, or legal meaning;
- exact-fidelity source content is simplified without authorized review;
- personal, restricted, confidential, or licensed content lacks approved handling;
- accessibility requirements or channel limitations cannot be met;
- the audience, channel, timing, or frequency creates material risk;
- evidence conflicts or is outdated;
- a material post-approval change occurs;
- a launch is requested without human authorization;
- measurement would collect data without defined authority, purpose, or retention handling.

## Deactivation and transition

EngagementOps closes or transitions to sustainment when the approved end condition is met, the initiative is withdrawn, the call to action expires, the audience need changes materially, or risk authority stops the work.

Closure records the final disposition, active assets and channels, results available at that time, unresolved limitations, ownership, retention or retirement actions, and any handoff back to EnablementOps. Engagement activity alone does not establish capability or business impact.
