# EnablementOps v1.0.0 release FAQ

## What is EnablementOps?

A governed operating system for coordinating enablement work across intake, design, production, delivery, measurement, maintenance, and governance.

## When should I use it?

Use it when planning or governing an enablement initiative, its assets, quality controls, lifecycle, or related handoffs.

## Is EngagementOps always required?

No. EngagementOps is optional and activates only when awareness, launch, participation, coordinated channels, or sustained engagement is required.

## Does EngagementOps own learning assets?

No. EnablementOps owns capability strategy, and LACE governs learning-asset construction and asset-specific quality assurance.
