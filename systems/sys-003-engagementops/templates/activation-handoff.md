# SYS-002 to SYS-003 activation handoff

Use with the canonical activation-handoff schema.

## Record
Handoff ID, version, source system, target system, status.

## Request
Initiative and proposed campaign IDs, approved outcome, intended behavior, included/excluded audiences, activation trigger, call to action, timing, dependencies.

## Sources
Authoritative sources, source owner, verification date.

## Constraints
Risk tier, material constraints, required reviews, measurement limits.

## Governance and disposition
Requester, receiver, decision authority, accepted/returned/declined decision, decision date, material gaps, nonmaterial gaps, and evidence.

Acceptance authorizes planning only.
