# EngagementOps asset specification

Use with the canonical asset-specification schema. The specification defines campaign intent and channel requirements; LACE governs learning-asset construction when applicable.

Required sections:

1. Record — asset ID, campaign ID, version, status, owner.
2. Purpose — asset type, objective, audience.
3. Delivery — controlled channels, platform, format, orientation, runtime.
4. Message — core idea, hook, call to action, reinforcement prompt.
5. Content — sections and authoritative source references.
6. Visual — style and production direction.
7. Accessibility — captions, transcript, alt text, reduced-motion alternative, screen-reader notes.
8. Governance — F0–F3 fidelity, SME and specialist reviews, approval and approver.
9. Measurement — linked measure IDs and interpretation limitations.
10. Lifecycle — activation, expiration, replacement trigger, and disposition.
