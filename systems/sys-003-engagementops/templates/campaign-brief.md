# EngagementOps campaign brief

Use with the canonical campaign-record schema.

## Record
Campaign ID, version, status, owner, related initiative/system IDs.

## Activation
Approved outcome, engagement objective, included and excluded audiences, observable call to action, start/end dates, and activation trigger.

## Sources
Controlling sources, owners, versions/effective dates, verification date, conflicts, assumptions, and limitations.

## Strategy
Risk tier, smallest effective ecosystem, selection rationale, channel profiles, phase sequence, cadence, frequency controls, sustainment, and exit criteria.

## Touchpoints
For each touchpoint: permanent ID, phase, channel, asset IDs, message role, call to action, date/window, owner, dependencies, accessibility requirements, and status.

## Governance
Source, campaign, channel, accessibility, analytics, specialist, support, and release owners; privacy classification; triggered reviews; exceptions; exact approval scope.

## Measurement and lifecycle
Measure IDs, baseline status, collection authority, interpretation limits, next review, expiration, retention class, support, closure, supersession, and disposition.
