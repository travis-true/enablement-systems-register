# EngagementOps delivery-readiness record

Use with the canonical delivery-readiness schema.

Record the campaign/version and every required gate: source, technical, specialist, accessibility, brand, operational, measurement, and release. For each gate capture priority, result, reviewer, date, evidence, and Not Applicable rationale.

Also record defects and exceptions, screenshot/redaction review, rights evidence, post-approval changes, P0/P1 regression, affected reapprovals, and the final version-bound release decision.

No score or elapsed service target can override a failed P0 gate, missing specialist authority, open Blocker/Critical defect, or missing human approval.
