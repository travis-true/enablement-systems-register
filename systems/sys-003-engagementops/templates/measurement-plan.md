# EngagementOps measurement plan

Use with the canonical measurement-plan schema.

1. Record — plan ID, campaign ID, version, status, owner.
2. Purpose — engagement objective, decision supported, highest claim level.
3. Baseline — status, period, source, value, and rationale.
4. Measures — controlled IDs, targets, threshold authority, frequency, window, and limitations.
5. Collection — source, method, population, fields, aggregation, access, classification, retention, disposition.
6. Reporting — audience, cadence, freshness, missing-data rule, interpretation boundary.
7. Governance — analytics owner, program owner, privacy review, approval, approver.
8. Decision — outcome, date, owner, and evidence.

Do not select a higher measurement level merely because lower-level data are available. Each claim must resolve to evidence at its own level.
