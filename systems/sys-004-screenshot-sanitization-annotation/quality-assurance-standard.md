# Quality assurance standard

## Gate

A screenshot release requires all seven dimensions to pass:

1. Instructional value — the image reduces error or search effort.
2. Accuracy — product, platform, permission state, and visible result are verified.
3. Privacy — all visible and embedded content is approved or synthetic.
4. Legibility — essential content remains readable at final placement.
5. Annotation — targets, order, labels, and lines are unambiguous.
6. Accessibility — instructions, alt text, non-color cues, and contrast are sufficient.
7. Lifecycle — source, master, derivative, owner, version, verification date, and recapture trigger are recorded.

## Severity

- Blocker: privacy, security, legal, or authorization failure.
- Critical: materially false instruction or inaccessible essential content.
- Major: likely task failure, ambiguous annotation, or unreadable target.
- Minor: non-blocking consistency or polish defect.

Open Blocker, Critical, or Major defects prohibit release.

## Evidence

Record reviewer, date, checks, defects, disposition, and release decision. A warning may be accepted only with owner rationale and a dated correction or recapture trigger.
