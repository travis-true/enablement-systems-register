# WF-002 — Governed Training Asset Publication

## Trigger and outcome

An approved need and source set become a verified, accessible, approved, published, and measurable asset.

## Flow

1. Receive and clarify the request.
2. Retrieve approved sources.
3. Draft to the required format and audience.
4. Verify claims and source alignment.
5. Perform accessibility and visual QA.
6. Obtain required approval.
7. Publish to the approved channel.
8. Measure use and schedule maintenance.

## Exceptions

Stop publication when sources conflict, approval is missing, accessibility defects are material, or restricted content would reach an unapproved channel.
