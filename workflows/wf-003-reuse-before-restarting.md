# WF-003 — Reuse Before Restarting

## Trigger and outcome

A new request begins with discovery of existing work and ends with a documented reuse, adaptation, or new-build decision.

## Flow

1. Describe the need and search clues.
2. Search approved repositories and knowledge sources.
3. Inspect likely matches and their provenance.
4. Assess currency, authority, audience fit, and permissions.
5. Reuse or adapt the best source when fit for purpose.
6. Validate the result and preserve attribution.
7. Start new work only when the evidence supports it.
