# WF-004 — Market Opportunity Validation

## Purpose

Use live buyer-intent evidence to test a commercial learning-product opportunity before full production.

## Draft flow

1. Define the buyer, problem, and proposed outcome.
2. Harvest complaints and repeated unmet needs.
3. Review competitors, substitutes, positioning, and pricing.
4. Score urgency, frequency, willingness to pay, differentiation, reach, and build effort.
5. Run the smallest credible demand test.
6. Record evidence and make a proceed, revise, pause, or stop decision.

## Current gap

Finalize weights, minimum evidence, decision thresholds, and the monitoring cadence.
